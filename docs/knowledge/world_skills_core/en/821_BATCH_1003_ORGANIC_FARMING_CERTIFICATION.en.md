# BATCH 679: Organic Farming — Certification & Practices

**Scope:** certification, cover_crops, composting, pest_control, rotation, soil_biology, marketing

|---|---|---|---|---|
| organicfarm.ops.usda_certification_process | USDA Organic — Certification Process | METHOD | Сертификация органического производства (США). NOP (National Organic Program): 3-летний переходный период (no prohibited substances). Сертификатор (CCOF, OCIA, MOFGA): аккредитован USDA/AMS. Organic System Plan (OSP): годовой документ (план земли, inputs, production, sales). Inspection: ежегодная (on-farm), spot tests (soil, water, product). Prohibited substances: синтетические пестициды, fertilizers, GMO, irradiation, sewage sludge. Allowed substances: listed in NOP §205.601-205.606. | Transition costs: 3 years revenue loss (lower yields без chemistry, no premium until certified). |
| organicfarm.ops.cover_crop_selection | Cover Crops — Species Selection | METHOD | Покровные культуры. Бобовые (legumes): фиксируют азот (hairy vetch 90-200 кг N/га, crimson clover 60-100, winter peas). Злаки (grasses): snring rye, wheat (biomass, prevent erosion, suppress weeds). Crucifers: mustard, radish (biofumigation — выделяет glucosinolates, suppresses nematodes/pathogens). Mixtures: 2-4 видовые смеси = stacking functions (legume N + grass biomass + crucifer pest control). Termination: frost-kill, rolling-crimping (roller-crimper), или herbicide (non-organic). Timing: 4-6 weeks before killing frost for establishment. | Бrassica: allelopathic (подавляет следующую культуру если не разложится). |
| organicfarm.ops.vermicomposting | Vermicomposting — Worm Bin | METHOD | Вермикомпостирование. Worms: Eisenia fetida (red wigglers, не обычные earthworms). Bin: plastic/wood, дренаж, температура 15-25°C. Bedding: shredded paper/cardboard (initial), moist (not wet). Feedstock: food scraps (no meat/dairy, no onion/garlic), coffee grounds, shredded leaves. C:N ratio: balance carbon (paper) and nitrogen (scraps). Processing: worms digest material → vermicast (worm castings) = high-quality organic amendment. Harvest: migrate (new bedding одна сторона, harvest другую). Liquid (leachate): dilute 1:10, liquid fertilizer. | Vermicast: excellent plant-available nutrients + microorganism diversity. |
| organicfarm.ops.soil_health_indicators | Soil Health — Field Assessment | METHOD | Визуальная оценка здоровья почвы на поле. Earthworm count: 10+ на кубик 30×30×30 см = healthy. Slake test: кубик почвы в воду (здоровая с структурой держится, не растворяется). Soil structure: granular/crumb = excellent, massive block = poor. Colour: dark brown/black = organic matter, pale = low OM. Smell: earthy (geosmin from actinomycetes) = good, musty/sour = anaerobic problem. Penetrometer: soil resistance <300 кПа = no compaction. Cover: bare soil = exposed to erosion/heat loss. | Soil health test kit: Cornell Soil Health Assessment (cost-effective). |
| organicfarm.ops.pest_monitoring_thresholds | IPM Thresholds — Organic Monitoring | METHOD | Экономический порог вредоносности. Economic threshold (ET): плотность вредителей при которой стоимость ущерба > стоимость меры. Мониторинг: sticky traps (aphids, whitefly, thrips), pheromone traps (codling moth, gypsy moth), scouting (каждые 7-10 дней). Action threshold (AT): обычно 70-80% от ET. Допустимые organic вмешательства: pyrethrin/spinosad/Bt (listed), neem (listed), kaolin clay, copper/sulfur (limited use). Biocontrols: Trichogramma, lacewings, ladybugs. Delay action: if natural enemies sufficient. | Не всегда доходить до ET: если natural enemies активны, они могут контролировать ситуацию. |
