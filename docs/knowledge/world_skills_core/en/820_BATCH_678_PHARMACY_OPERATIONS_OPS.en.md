# BATCH 678: Pharmacy Operations — Community & Clinical

**KnowledgeUnits:** 50
**Namespace:** `pharmacyops.*`
**Scope:** dispensing, counseling, medication_reconciliation, compounding, OTC, HIPAA, inventory, drug_interaction

| ID | KnowledgeUnit | Тип | Суть | Практический смысл |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pharmacyops.ops.prescription_verification | Prescription — Verification Steps | METHOD | Проверка рецепта. Drug-Utilization Review (DUR): автоматическая проверка при вводе — drug-drug interactions (DDI), drug-disease contraindications, duplicate therapy, allergies (reported in profile), dose check (по age/weight). Clinical review: pharmacist reviews all alerts (не технику). HIPAA: разрешение пациента на shared profile. Controlled substances: DEA verification, ID check, e-prescribe (EPCS). Hard copy: handwritten — legibility check, signature. | ISMP (Institute for Safe Medication Practices): 10 high-alert medications (insulin, anticoagulants, opioids, concentrated electrolytes). |
| pharmacyops.ops.medication_counseling | Counseling — Prime Questions | METHOD | Консультирование пациента (Pharmacist obligation). Indian Health Service model «3 Prime Questions»: 1) Что врач сказал вам об этом лекарстве? 2) Как врач сказал принимать? 3) Что врач сказал об ожидаемом эффекте? Show-and-tell (patient repeat): «расскажите как будете принимать». Для новых рецептов: все 8 elements of counseling (indication, dose, storage, adverse effects, interactions, monitoring, refills). OBRA 90 mandate: offer counseling to ALL Medicaid patients. | Verbal counseling важнее leaflet (80% пациентов не читают). |
| pharmacyops.ops.drug_interaction_screening | Drug Interactions — Clinical Significance | METHOD | Клиническая значимость взаимодействий. Severity: Major (death/hospitalisation — warfarin+NSAIDs), Moderate (clinical effect possible — statins+macrolides), Minor (minimal clinical effect). Evidence quality: established, probable, suspected, possible, unlikely. Механизмы: pharmacokinetic (CYP450 — CYP3A4 inhibitor, CYP2D6 poor metabolizer), pharmacodynamic (additive CNS depression, bleeding risk). Contraindicated combinations (never use): MAOIs + SSRIs (serotonin syndrome), statins + gemfibrozil (rhabdomyolysis risk), certain QT-prolonging combinations. | Не все alerts clinically significant (alert fatigue — 80%+ ignored). Context is key. |
| pharmacyops.ops.medication_reconciliation | Med Reconciliation — Transitions of Care | METHOD | Сверка лекарств при переводах. Best Possible Medication History (BPMH): сравнить prescriptions от нескольких источников (пациент+семья, pharmacy profile, hospital records, PCP records). Discrepancies: intentional (врач отменил что-то) vs unintentional (ошибка). Omission (пропущено), commission (добавлено лишнее), wrong dose/frequency. Discharge reconciliation: новая instructional list = BPMH + hospital changes. Follow-up: pharmacy call 48-72 часов (readmission prevention). | High-risk patients (polypharmacy ≥5 drugs, elderly, multiple providers): priority for reconciliation. |
| pharmacyops.ops.compounding_sterile_prep | Sterile Compounding — USP 797 | METHOD | Стерильное приготовление в аптеке. USP 797: federal standard для sterile compounding. Buffer room: ISO 7 (Class 10000), positive pressure для non-hazardous. Hazardous drugs (antineoplastics, antivirals): ISO 7 with negative pressure, separate area. PEC (Primary Engineering Control): ISO 5 LAF workbench (non-hazardous) или BSC Class II B2 (hazardous). Gowning: double gloves, sterile gown, mask, eye protection. Aseptic technique: critical site protection, never break sterile field. Beyond-use dating: storage conditions dependent (по 797 tables). Media fill: personnel qualification (growth promotion test for process). | USP 797 compliance: state boards enforce, failed inspections → closure. |
