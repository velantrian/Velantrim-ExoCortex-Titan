| healthpolicy.ops.aca_provisions | ACA — Affordable Care Act | METHOD | Закон о доступном медицинском обслуживании (ACA, 2010): Ключевые положения: расширение Medicaid (до 138% FPL), страховые биржи (Marketplaces), запрет отказа по pre-existing conditions, обязательное покрытие preventive services, молодые до 26 лет на родительской страховке. Penalty (Individual Mandate): убрана в 2019. | «Donut Hole»: разрыв в Medicare Part D покрытии (ACA закрывает). |
| healthpolicy.ops.global_health_systems | Health Systems — WHO Framework | METHOD | Фреймворк здравоохранительных систем (ВОЗ): 6 Building Blocks: Service delivery, Healthcare workforce, Information, Medical products + technologies, Financing, Leadership/Governance. Health outcomes: improved health, responsiveness, financial protection. | Bismarck model (Germany, Japan): обязательное страхование через работодателя. Beveridge (UK, Sweden): государственная система. Natl Health Insurance (Canada). Out-of-pocket. |
| healthpolicy.ops.health_disparities | Health Disparities — Framework | METHOD | Неравенство в здоровье: Disparities: racial/ethnic, socioeconomic, geographic (rural-urban). Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ): NHDR annual. Healthy People 2030: устранение disparities. SDOH: education, economic stability, neighborhood, social context, healthcare access. | ACES (Adverse Childhood Experiences): связь SDOH + health outcomes в будущем. |
| healthpolicy.ops.mental_health_parity | Mental Health Parity — MHPAEA | METHOD | Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA, 2008): требует страховщиков покрывать MH/SUD не строже, чем физическое здоровье. No-Surprise Act: аналогично для кризисных услуг. Mental health workforce shortage. Collaborative care model: BHI в первичной помощи. Measurement-based care (MBC). | «Любая mental health condition»: по сравнению с physical. Финансовые требования + treatment limits. |
| healthpolicy.ops.preventive_services | Preventive Care — USPSTF | METHOD | Профилактические услуги (USPSTF): Grade A/B: должны покрываться страховщиками. Grade C: рассмотреть. Grade D: рекомендовано против. Grade I: недостаточно доказательств. ACA: Grade A/B бесплатно без deductible. 2022 Braidwood: правовой вызов мандату. | Braden v. HHS (2022): суды оспаривают ACA Preventive Care бесплатное покрытие. |
| healthpolicy.ops.hospital_quality_measures | Hospital Quality — CMS | METHOD | Качество медицинской помощи (CMS): Hospital Compare: публичное сравнение больниц. Merit-based Incentive Payment System (MIPS): physician payment. Bundled Payments for Care Improvement (BPCI). VBP (Value-Based Purchasing): incentives for outcomes. HAC reduction: Hospital-Acquired Conditions. | Pay-for-performance: вместо Fee-for-Service. Shift к outcomes vs volume. |
| healthpolicy.ops.gun_violence_public_health | Firearm Injury — Public Health | METHOD | Огнестрельное насилие как проблема общественного здоровья: США: 40 000+ смертей/год (50% suicides). Surgeon General's first Advisory (2024): gun violence = public health crisis. Red Flag Laws (ERPO). Extreme Risk Protection Orders. Universal Background Checks. Firearm safe storage counseling in pediatrics. | Исследования по огнестрельному оружию: CDC funding запрет (Dickey Amendment) 1996-2019. |
| healthpolicy.ops.workforce_healthcare | Healthcare Workforce — Shortage | METHOD | Нехватка медицинского персонала: Projected shortage: 37 000-124 000 physicians by 2034 (AAMC). Primary care gap: большинство нехваток. HPSA (Health Professional Shortage Area). NHSC (National Health Service Corps): loan repayment. International Medical Graduates (IMGs): 25% US physicians. | Scope of Practice expansion: NPs + PAs → allow more independence. |
| healthpolicy.ops.global_drug_pricing | Drug Pricing — International | METHOD | Международное ценообразование лекарств: USA: highest prices globally. Reference pricing (Ger): AMNOG assessment. Universal pricing (UK): NICE evaluation. International Reference Pricing: Biden IRA §1194 (Medicare drug negotiation). Insulin analogs: $300/vial (USA) vs $10 (Canada). | IRA 2022: первое разрешение Medicare напрямую договариваться о ценах. |
| healthpolicy.ops.telehealth_reimbursement | Telehealth — Reimbursement Policy | METHOD | Оплата телемедицины: Medicare coverage expanded (COVID, 2020). Ryan Haight Act: controlled substance prescribing via telehealth (waived during COVID). Current status: extension through 2026. Originating site limitations: Rural area restrictions reduced. CPT codes: 99441-99443 (telephone). | Telehealth equity: rural + low-income benefit most. Digital divide = barrier. |
| healthpolicy.ops.rural_health_policy | Rural Health — Policy | METHOD | Политика здоровья в сельских районах: Rural Health Clinics (RHCs): enhanced reimbursement. Critical Access Hospitals (CAH): Medicare cost-based reimbursement. USDA Rural Health Programs. Health disparities: higher mortality from chronic disease. Maternal mortality: higher rural. | Rural hospital closures: 150+ since 2010. Healthcare desert creation. |
| healthpolicy.ops.homelessness_health | Homelessness — Health Outcomes | METHOD | Здоровье бездомных: Homeless individuals: 5-10× higher hospitalization rates. 30-40% have mental illness. 25-50% substance use disorder. HCH (Health Care for the Homeless): Ryan White CARE Act programs. Medical respite programs: post-hospital care. Housing First model: снижает hospitalization more than treatment-first. | Permanent Supportive Housing: наиболее эффективное вмешательство. |
| healthpolicy.ops.climate_health_policy | Climate-Health — Policy | METHOD | Климат-здоровье политика: EPA Clean Air Act: регулирование выбросов для здоровья. EPA finding (2009): GHGs = threat to public health. Paris Agreement + health linkage. Climate change medicine: NEJM 2020 series. Health in NDCs: все больше стран включают health в NDCs. | Co-benefits: снижение загрязнения воздуха от декарбонизации = немедленная польза здоровью. |
| healthpolicy.ops.maternal_mortality_policy | Maternal Mortality — Policy | METHOD | Материнская смертность: USA: 23.8/100 000 (2020, CDC) — выше среди развитых стран. Racial disparity: Black women 2.9× higher. MMR для Black women: 55.3 vs White: 19.1. Maternal Mortality Review Committees (MMRCs): каждый штат. Preventability: 84% of deaths preventable. 4th trimester: 30-45 дней после родов = high risk period. | Основные причины: кровотечение, кардиоваскулярные, инфекция, тромбоэмболия. |
