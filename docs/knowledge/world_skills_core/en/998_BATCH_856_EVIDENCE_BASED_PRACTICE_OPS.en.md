# BATCH 856: Clinical Decision Support — Evidence-Based Practice

**KnowledgeUnits:** 50
**Namespace:** `evidencebased.ops.*`
**Scope:** PICO_framework, literature_appraisal, clinical_guidelines, grading_evidence, evidence_implementation, clinical_reasoning, diagnostic_accuracy

| ID | KnowledgeUnit | Тип | Суть | Практический смысл |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| evidbased.ops.pico_framework | PICO — Clinical Question Framework | METHOD | PICO: структурирование клинического вопроса. P (Population/Problem): кто пациент? I (Intervention): что рассматривается? C (Comparison): с чем сравнивать? O (Outcome): что важно? Варианты: PICOT (T=Time), PICOS (S=Study Design). PubMed: MeSH terms по PICO. | Плохой PICO → нерелевантный поиск. Хороший PICO → targeted search + critical appraisal. |
| evidbased.ops.levels_evidence | Levels of Evidence — Oxford | METHOD | Уровни доказательности (Oxford CEBM 2011): Level 1a: SR of RCTs. Level 1b: индивидуальный RCT. Level 2a: SR когортных. Level 2b: когортное. Level 3a: SR case-control. Level 3b: индивидуальный case-control. Level 4: Case series. Level 5: Expert opinion. GRADE (WHO/Cochrane): High-Moderate-Low-Very Low. | GRADE: более нюансированный (учитывает indirect, inconsistent evidence). |
| evidbased.ops.grade_certainty | GRADE — Certainty Assessment | METHOD | GRADE (Grading of Recommendations Assessment): Certainty grades: High (RCT = high start) → downgrade if: риск ошибок, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias. Observational = low start → upgrade if: large effect, dose-response, plausible confounding. Strong vs Conditional recommendation. | Conditional recommendation: «We suggest» vs Strong «We recommend». |
| evidbased.ops.critical_appraisal_rct | Critical Appraisal — RCT | METHOD | Критическая оценка RCT: CONSORT checklist. Internal validity: randomization, allocation concealment, blinding, completeness. External validity: representative population. Selection bias (antes de randomizar). Attrition bias (post). Performance bias. Detection bias. Reporting bias. | Allocation concealment ≠ blinding. Concealment предотвращает selection bias до назначения. |
| evidbased.ops.clinical_guidelines | Clinical Guidelines — Development | METHOD | Разработка клинических рекомендаций: GRADE process. Multidisciplinary panel. Conflict of interest declaration. Systematic review. Clinical + patient perspectives. External review. Regular update. Implementation toolkit. NICE Technology Appraisals: health economics + clinical. | «Strong recommendation with weak evidence»: редкость — обычно обратное соотношение. |
| evidbased.ops.likelihood_ratios | Diagnostic Accuracy — LRs | METHOD | Вероятностные соотношения (LR): LR+ = Sensitivity / (1 - Specificity). LR- = (1 - Sensitivity) / Specificity. Pre-test probability → Post-test probability: через Bayes или Fagan nomogram. LR+ > 10: strong confirmation. LR- < 0.1: strong exclusion. | Streptococcal pharyngitis: Centor Score = pre-test probability. |
| evidbased.ops.number_needed_harm | NNH — Safety Metric | METHOD | Число, нужное для вреда (NNH): NNH = 1/ARI. ARI = Rate_treated - Rate_control. Важен для benefit/risk balance. Treating 100 patients with statins: 1 event prevented (NNT=100) vs 1 muscle problem (NNH=100). Benefit:Harm ratio context. | Aspirin for primary prevention: NNT vs NNH = почти равны → не рекомендуется рутинно. |
| evidbased.ops.clinical_prediction_rule | Clinical Prediction Rule — Validation | METHOD | Клинические прогностические правила: derivation (разработка модели), validation (внешняя проверка). WELLS score (DVT), Ottawa rules. AUC (AUROC): discrimination. Calibration: predicted vs observed. Hosmer-Lemeshow test. Decision impact studies: изменяет ли реальное поведение. | Ottawa Ankle Rules: validation многократная → высокая уверенность. |
| evidbased.ops.meta_analysis_heterogeneity | Meta-Analysis — Heterogeneity | METHOD | Гетерогенность в мета-анализе: I² статистика: 0% = нет, 25% = низкая, 50% = умеренная, 75% = высокая. Q-test: статистический тест. Subgroup analysis: объяснение. Random effects vs Fixed effects model: при наличии гетерогенности → Random effects. Funnel plot: publication bias. | При I² > 75%: мета-анализ сомнительной ценности. Лучше: narratively synthesize. |
| evidbased.ops.shared_decision_making | Shared Decision Making | METHOD | Совместное принятие решений (SDM): evidence + patient preferences + clinical expertise. «Option talk»: обсудить все варианты. «Decision talk»: определить предпочтения. Patient Decision Aids: IPDAS standards. Question Prompt Lists. «Three-talk model» (Elwyn). | SDM instruments: Ottawa Decision Support Framework. |
| evidbased.ops.clinical_practice_guidelines_appraisal | Guideline Appraisal — AGREE | METHOD | AGREE II (Appraisal of Guidelines for Research and Evaluation): 6 domains. 1) Scope + Purpose. 2) Stakeholder Involvement. 3) Rigor of Development. 4) Clarity of Presentation. 5) Applicability. 6) Editorial Independence. 23 items. | AGREE II score < 40%: слабое качество руководства (не следует слепо). |
| evidbased.ops.implementation_science | Implementation Science — CFIR | METHOD | Наука реализации (Implementation Science): разрыв между доказательствами и практикой. CFIR (Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research): 5 domains. Intervention characteristics, Outer setting, Inner setting, Characteristics of individuals, Process. Normalisation Process Theory. RE-AIM: Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation, Maintenance. | «Knowing is not doing»: 17-year gap знание → практика. Implementation science устраняет. |
| evidbased.ops.qualitative_evidence_synthesis | Qualitative Evidence Synthesis | METHOD | Синтез качественных доказательств: Meta-ethnography (Noblit & Hare): синтез интерпретаций. Thematic synthesis. Framework synthesis. Grounded theory synthesis. Thomas & Harden: systematic reviews of qualitative research. GRADE-CERQual: оценка уверенности в качественных. | Qualitative findings → implementation insights. Why does something not work? |
| evidbased.ops.patient_reported_outcomes | Patient-Reported Outcomes — PRO | METHOD | Исходы, сообщаемые пациентом (PRO): прямая оценка пациентом симптомов + функций + QoL без интерпретации. PROMIS (NIH): item bank + adaptive testing. SF-36: классический. EQ-5D: экономический. PRO-CTCAE (onkology). FDA guidance: PRO in clinical trials. | FDA требует PRO для labeling claims + clinical meaningfulness. |
